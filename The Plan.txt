14/9 - Architecture & Loss Overhaul:
1. Red-Only Target Supervision: Output head predicts (B, 5, 5) exclusively for the 5 Red enemy roles.
   Stops wasting 50% of gradients on trivial Blue identity copying.
2. FoW Importance Weighting: Multiplies loss by 3x on frames where enemy was hidden in FoW (conf==0 at t=59).
3. Auxiliary L1 Coordinate Penalty: Added direct L1 distance penalty on mu (weight 5.0) to prevent
   the model from cheating by placing mu in the center with huge sigma.
4. Visualizer: Plots Blue team known positions from t=59, and renders 2-sigma ellipses + ground truth error lines for Red team.
